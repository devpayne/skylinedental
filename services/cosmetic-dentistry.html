Some of Our Cosmetic Dentistry Services

A treatment for every kind of smile

Veneers

Slender porcelain strips that adhere to the visible side of a tooth to fix chips, cracks, and uneven spacing — a straighter, brighter smile without braces or bleaching.

Learn more →

Teeth Whitening

Our most common cosmetic procedure — highly effective whitening treatments for our Brigham City, Tremonton, and Willard-area patients.

Crowns & Bridges

Permanent, tooth-shaped replacements bonded to adjacent teeth — protecting the bone structure of the mouth while filling in for missing or fractured teeth.

Full Dentures

Affordable, custom-fitted smile substitutes that stay durable and feel natural, without impeding your speech or bite.

Learn more →

Dental Implants

Solidly anchored in your jaw so they look and feel like a natural part of your tooth line.
