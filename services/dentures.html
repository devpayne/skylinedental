Full & Partial Dentures

Durable, attractive, and affordable: custom dentures that bridge the gaps missing teeth leave behind.

What Dentures Are Good For

A full set of teeth makes a big difference

It's certainly possible to go about your daily life with missing teeth, but gaps can present challenges when it comes to eating and fully participating in conversations and social activities. Durable and attractive, full or partial dentures can go a long way toward bridging those gaps, improving both your smile and your future.

Dentures aren't just a solution for missing teeth. They can also be the right route for chipped, cracked, or broken teeth. When you come in, Dr. Olson will sit down for a careful consultation to determine the best course of action, so you can leave feeling confident you've been given all the information to make the right choice. If you already have dentures that need relining or repair, we provide those services too.

How dentures work

Dentures begin with a custom-fitted acrylic base molded either to the roof of your mouth or around the bottom teeth in a semicircle. Replacement teeth fit into the base to complete your smile, shaped with the same custom attention so they match the size, shape, and appearance of the rest of your teeth.

If oral surgery or removal of a damaged tooth is necessary, we'll wait for it to heal before placing the dentures, though immediate emergency dentures are available in many cases. It may take a week or two to get used to the feel of dentures, and then you'll be right at home eating and speaking with them in.

Are dentures affordable?

In many cases, your dentures will be covered or mostly covered by your insurance policy. We work with many insurers to optimize your out-of-pocket cost. Fitted properly the first time, dentures will last for years to come, and compared to dental implants, which can cost upward of $5,000, the total cost of dentures is usually a fraction of that.
