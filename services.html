Our Services

Dr. Aaron M. Olson offers a full range of dental services in Brigham City — including sleep dentistry for patients nervous about implants, tooth-colored fillings, crowns, bridges, laser procedures, and dentures.

Northern Utah's full menu of modern dental care, from routine prevention to complete smile transformations.

Periodontics

Preventing, diagnosing, and treating gum disease and oral inflammation — plus expert implant placement.

Dental Implants

A complete, root-to-crown replacement for damaged teeth — more comfortable and natural-looking than the alternatives.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Cavities filled comfortably, with material that blends right in with your smile.

Veneers

Slim, custom porcelain shells that reshape and brighten teeth to fix a wide range of smile issues.

Crowns

Durable, permanent tooth restorations — Dr. Olson knows exactly when and how to use them.

Bridges

A fixed way to fill gaps from missing teeth, precisely fitted for a natural, comfortable bite.

Wisdom Tooth Removal

Comfortable extraction of impacted wisdom teeth before they cause pain, swelling, or crowding.

Laser Procedures

Precise, state-of-the-art laser treatment for decay removal, gum reshaping, and faster whitening.

Oral Sedation

Several sedation options for nervous patients — we'll help you find the right fit for your visit.
