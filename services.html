What We Offer

Our Services

Dr. Aaron M. Olson offers a full range of dental services in Brigham City — including sleep dentistry for patients nervous about implants, tooth-colored fillings, crowns, bridges, laser procedures, and dentures.

We are proud to serve Northern Utah, including Brigham City, Tremonton, and surrounding areas. Below is a closer look at each of the services we provide — reach out any time if you have questions about which option is right for you.

Periodontics

We specialize in the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of periodontal disease, and in the placement of dental implants. We are also experts in treating oral inflammation.

Dental Implants

We provide state-of-the-art implants that serve as a complete replacement for your damaged teeth, all the way down to the root. This is often the ideal solution for tooth decay, gum disease, injury, or other tooth problems involving the root. Our implants improve speech and bite, while being more comfortable and attractive than many alternatives.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

A basic staple of dentist offices everywhere, we make filling cavities for our Brigham City-area patients as comfortable as possible. Our friendly staff will make sure you and your family get the complex comprehensive treatment you need.

Veneers

A dental veneer is a slender, tooth-shaped piece of material that adheres to the outward-facing side of your tooth to enhance its attractiveness. Porcelain veneers from our Brigham City office can fix many types of tooth problems.

Crowns

Dr. Olson has extensive experience placing crowns just right, as well as knowing when they're the best tool for the situation. If what you need is a permanent tooth replacement, we'll get on the job right away.

Bridges

These permanent dental restorations join an artificial tooth to the teeth next door. They're a great way to fill the spaces where missing teeth once were, and sometimes involve crowns on the adjacent teeth. A lot goes into making sure bridge measurements are perfect so you experience no discomfort or unnatural bite.

Wisdom Tooth Removal

Wisdom teeth usually appear in the late teens or early twenties, but may fail to erupt due to lack of room in the jaw. When a wisdom tooth is impacted, it may need to be removed — otherwise you may develop gum tenderness, swelling, or even severe pain. At Skyline Dental, we make sure this process is as comfortable as possible.

Laser Procedures

Lasers are increasingly used in dental procedures — removing tooth decay, preparing teeth for fillings, curing a filling once it's in place, reshaping gums and removing gum bacteria, and speeding up teeth whitening. Our state-of-the-art equipment means you're in competent, caring hands.

Oral Sedation

Several sedation methods are available for patients who are nervous about the dentist's office. Please talk to us about your concerns when you make an appointment or when you arrive, so we can determine which version of oral sedation is right for you.
