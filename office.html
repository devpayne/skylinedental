Our Office

Our professional dental office is conveniently located on Medical Drive and accessible via all major roads — fully equipped with state-of-the-art technology and a friendly, knowledgeable staff.

We are proud of our office, which fully utilizes state-of-the-art equipment, including digital X-rays. We also have a modern sterilization system that surpasses OSHA requirements.

Our friendly and knowledgeable staff is dedicated to your comfort and quality care. We provide the best and latest in dental technology and treatment options, and gentle, family-oriented dental care to the adults and children of this community.

In addition to general dentistry and cosmetic dentistry, we offer periodontics (the structures supporting the teeth), implants, endodontics (root canal treatments), and more. We emphasize comprehensive quality care and preventive dentistry.

We look forward to welcoming you to our beautiful dental office in Brigham City!

If, for any reason, you cannot keep a scheduled appointment or will be delayed, please call us as soon as possible.
Insurance & Payment

Ways to pay for your care

Insurance

We accept most traditional insurance plans. Contact our office to verify acceptance of your plan. Skyline Dental does not participate in Health Management Organizations, but we're happy to file your insurance claims for you.

Payment Options

We accept checks, cash, and credit cards, and offer a flexible payment plan — please see us for details. We're happy to file insurance for your reimbursement as long as you're free to choose your own dentist.

CareCredit

CareCredit helps patients pay for treatments their insurance doesn't cover, with no-interest financing and low minimum monthly-payment options. Apply in three simple steps with instant approval at carecredit.com.
