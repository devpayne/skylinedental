Meet Dr. Aaron M. Olson

Dr. Olson's Story

A hometown dentist serving Northern Utah

Skyline Dental is the dental office of Dr. Aaron M. Olson. Located in Brigham City, we service the Tremonton, Bear River City, Perry, Willard, Mantua, and North Ogden areas — with patients ranging all the way to New York City.

Dr. Olson was born and raised in Brigham City and is a 3rd-generation graduate of Box Elder High School. After completing his undergraduate studies at the University of Utah, he attended The Ohio State College of Dentistry.

Upon completion of his Doctorate of Dental Surgery degree — graduating Magna Cum Laude — Dr. Olson attended a one-year general dentistry residency at the Lutheran Medical Center located in Mesa, Arizona. During his year of post-doctoral education, he studied advanced procedures in general dentistry with an emphasis on complex comprehensive treatment and dental implant therapy.
